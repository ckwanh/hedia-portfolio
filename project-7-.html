A cozy craft café for knitters and sewers

Little Knit is a cozy craft café where you can knit, sew, chat and buy yarn by weight. Make something, your way.

My Branding

Idea: a cozy, welcoming space for knitters and sewers to gather, learn and share. The brand is warm, friendly and approachable, with a focus on community and creativity.

The challenge: Creating a brand that reflects the warmth and creativity of the community while being distinct and memorable.

ytd

What I did

  • Researched the craft café market and identified key competitors and trends.
  • Designed the brand identity, including logo, color palette, typography, and visual elements.
  • Built the brand guidelines and created mockups for various applications, including signage, packaging, and social media.
  • Revised the designs based on feedback from peers and mentors, ensuring the brand was cohesive and effective.

Typography

The primary typeface is a rounded sans-serif, which is friendly and approachable. The secondary typeface is a serif, which adds a touch of elegance and sophistication.

The final design

The final design is a cohesive brand identity that reflects the warmth and creativity of the Little Knit community. The logo is simple and memorable, the color palette is warm and inviting, and the typography is friendly and approachable.

What I learned

This project taught me the importance of research and iteration in the design process. By understanding the target audience and analyzing competitors, I was able to create a brand that stands out while still being relevant to the market. Additionally, I learned how to effectively communicate my design decisions through mockups and presentations.

Logo Animation

The logo animation is a simple, yet effective way to bring the brand to life. The animation starts with the logo appearing on screen, then the elements of the logo animate in a playful and engaging way. The animation is designed to be used in various contexts, such as social media, presentations, and videos.
